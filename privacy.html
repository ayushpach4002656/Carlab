PRIVACY.

CarLab Launch Edition stores your garage, profile, XP and preferences locally in your browser using localStorage. This launch build does not require an account and does not send those saved builds to a CarLab server.

If analytics, advertising, accounts, payments or email collection are added later, this notice must be updated before those systems are enabled.

CarLab scores and vehicle values are entertainment/game values and are not official manufacturer specifications or live market valuations.
