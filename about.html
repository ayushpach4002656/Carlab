WHY CARLAB

BUILD. BATTLE. FLEX.

CarLab is an independent enthusiast playground for building, comparing and collecting dream cars. The launch 3D vehicles are original generic archetypes, not manufacturer-licensed digital twins.

Vehicle and brand names in the catalog are used to organize the enthusiast game experience. Names and trademarks belong to their respective owners. CarLab is not affiliated with or endorsed by those manufacturers.
